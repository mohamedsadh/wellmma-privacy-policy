This Privacy Policy explains how Wellmma: Re-Habit collects, processes, stores, and protects information when you use the application. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about how the app operates.

1. Introduction

Wellmma: Re-Habit is designed to help users build healthier habits, improve productivity, monitor screen time, organize daily tasks, manage medicine reminders, track fitness activities, plan wellness journeys, and support personal growth.

Most features are designed to work locally on your device, allowing you to use the application without unnecessarily sharing your personal information.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be processed:

  • Habit tracking information you create.
  • To-do lists and personal plans.
  • Medicine reminder schedules.
  • Fitness and wellness tracking records.
  • Screen time statistics generated from your device.
  • Business planning information created by you.
  • Application preferences and settings.
  • Premium purchase or subscription status provided by Google Play.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties.

3. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Allows the application to calculate screen time and provide digital wellness insights. Usage statistics are processed on your device.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES (if requested) Used only to identify installed applications for accurate screen time reporting. The installed application list is not sold or shared.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows habit reminders, medicine reminders, wellness notifications, and productivity notifications.
INTERNET Required only for features that depend on online services, such as Google Play Billing or future online functionality.

4. Screen Time Information

If you enable Screen Time Tracking, the application reads Android Usage Statistics after you grant permission. The purpose is solely to help you understand your device usage and improve your digital habits. This information is processed locally whenever possible and is not used for advertising.

5. Medicine Reminder

Medicine reminders are provided solely to help users remember scheduled medications. The application does not verify prescriptions and does not provide medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical decisions.

6. AI Features

Some features may provide AI-assisted suggestions related to habits, productivity, wellness planning, or general educational information. AI-generated suggestions are intended for informational purposes only. They should not be considered professional medical, legal, financial, psychological, or business advice.

7. Google Play Billing

Premium subscriptions, one-time purchases, and lifetime purchases are securely processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details. Payment information is handled entirely by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Storage

Most user-created information is stored locally on your device. Examples include:

  • Habit records
  • To-do lists
  • Medicine reminders
  • Fitness logs
  • Business plans
  • Application settings
  • Screen time summaries

If optional cloud synchronization or online backup features are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features become available.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play Store
  • Google Play Billing
  • Android system services required for application functionality

If additional third-party services are integrated in future updates, they will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your Rights

  • Delete locally stored application data through your device settings.
  • Uninstall the application whenever you choose.
  • Contact us regarding privacy-related questions.
